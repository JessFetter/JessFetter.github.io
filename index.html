Hi, I’m Jess Fetter

A Mechanical Engineering Student at Purdue University

I’m a mechanical engineering student who loves building real, functional things—whether that’s refining injection‑moldable parts, machining CNC components, or developing Python tools that turn ideas into usable systems. I’ve worked across the full design‑to‑fabrication pipeline, from CAD and DFM to CAM and hands‑on machining, and I enjoy projects that blend creativity with engineering rigor.

Experience

Summer Engineering Intern

At Upper Story, I transformed a rough toy car concept into a manufacturable multi‑part assembly, refining geometry, designing snap‑fits, and applying DFM principles. The internship strengthened my ability to turn early design ideas into production‑ready components.

Peer Teacher

As a Peer Teacher in Purdue’s First‑Year Engineering program, I help student teams navigate design challenges and apply tools like Python, MATLAB, and Excel effectively. The role has strengthened my technical communication and my ability to guide beginners through complex engineering concepts with clarity and confidence.

Lifeguard

At Purdue Aquatics, I maintain patron safety and uphold strict operational standards during recreational swim hours. The role reinforces my attention to detail, quick decision‑making, and ability to stay focused in high‑responsibility environments.

Projects

CNC-Machined Chess Set

I’m machining a full chess set in the Bechtel Makerspace, producing aluminum pawns and rooks through CNC lathe operations and a second‑op CNC mill setup. I generated all Fusion 360 CAM toolpaths myself, focusing on precision, repeatability, and clean surface finishes across each piece.

Amphibious Drone(Vertically Integrated Projects)

In Purdue’s Amphibious Drone VIP team, I lead coordination across mechanical, CFD, and test‑rig subgroups to keep development aligned and moving. Tapestry Crochet Project

I built a Python program that converts any image into a simplified, stitch‑accurate tapestry crochet pattern using K‑means color clustering and custom interpolation methods. The tool generates a complete, human‑readable PDF by automating color labeling, zig‑zag row traversal, palette mapping, and pattern formatting.

4 X 8 Hobbyist CNC

I built and assembled a 4×8 CNC based on the Lowrider L3 design, machining the MDF struts using the partially completed machine itself. Along the way, I solved CAM and G‑code issues—like correcting a zeroing error that caused the toolpath to plunge into the spoilboard—while working through the challenges of a large-format DIY build.
